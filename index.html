About

A little context.

I'm currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Molecular and Cellular Biology at Heidelberg University. My background is in molecular biology, with hands-on experience in RNA structure probing, membrane biochemistry, and data analysis.

I've worked on RNA viruses and RNA structure at IBMC Strasbourg and HIRI Würzburg, and on mitochondrial and ER membrane proteins at BZH Heidelberg and Saarland University.

Research projects

2026 · Biochemistry Center Heidelberg (BZH)

Mitochondrial lipid transport

Investigated how Ups1 interacts with mitochondrial membranes and how phosphatidic acid is transported during cardiolipin biosynthesis.

2026 · IBMC Strasbourg

RNA structure and dynamics in retroviruses

Studied structural correlations in retroviral RRE RNA and tRNA dynamics using nanopore direct RNA sequencing, Nano-DMS-MaP, and data analysis.

2025 · Saarland University / UKS Homburg

PERK activation in the unfolded protein response

HIV ribosomal frameshifting

Used cloning, Western blotting, and cell culture while contributing to the identification of a potential novel ribosomal frameshift site in HIV.

Current research and side projects.

Current project · with Angelita, IBMC Strasbourg

rG4 structure from chemical probing

I analyzed nanopore DMS-MaP data for the HIF1A 5′UTR RNA G-quadruplex (rG4), comparing wild-type and G-tract mutant constructs at pH 5 and 8 using SEISMIC-RNA.

Plans

  • Add an msDMS-MaP N7-G readout to measure guanine Hoogsteen engagement directly.
  • Test K⁺/Li⁺ conditions and WT/mutant constructs.
  • Use the reactivity to calibrate rG4 folding and test structure-prediction models.
